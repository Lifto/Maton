# User

Who is my user? This file is where I record what I learn about them.

## Discovery Questions

- What does my user care most about?
- What are their ongoing commitments and responsibilities?
- What kind of work do they do?
- What are they currently struggling with?
- What does a good day look like for them?
- What do they want more of in their life?
- What drains them?
- How do they prefer to communicate?
